Delete Your TideCraftAI Account

You can delete your account directly from within the app:

  1. Open the TideCraftAI app
  2. Go to the Profile page
  3. Tap on the “Delete Account” button

What is deleted?

  • ✅ Your login account (email/password)
  • ❌ Some generated content (e.g., notes, text) may not be deleted automatically

If you want us to delete all your stored content as well, please contact us at tidecraftai@gmail.com.

This page is provided to comply with Google Play’s Data Deletion policy.
